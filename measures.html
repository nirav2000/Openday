01 · THE DIFFERENCES

Four scores that answer different questions

Attainment 8How many eligible GCSE grade-points did pupils earn in designated slots?Usually 0–90*
Progress 8How much better or worse did pupils do than peers with similar KS2 starting scores?Centred around 0
EBacc APSWhat were grades across English, maths, two science positions, humanities and language?Usually 0–9*
Sixth-form GCSE APSWhat was the average of the applicant's GCSE/IGCSE grades?Usually 0–9*

*For ordinary grade 9–1 GCSEs. Other approved qualifications, including some level 3 qualifications, may yield different accountability points. These are not all interchangeable forms of APS.

02 · ATTAINMENT

Attainment 8: eight qualification slots, ten weighted grade positions

English and mathematics occupy one subject slot each but normally contribute double points. Three slots are reserved for eligible EBacc subjects (science, computer science, history, geography or languages); three are open slots for other eligible GCSEs or approved qualifications. Any unused slot adds zero. The three EBacc slots do not require a language or humanities subject.

English × 218Best English grade, double if both Language and Literature taken
Maths × 218Eligible GCSE Maths grade doubled
3 EBacc slots27Three best eligible science, language and humanities grades
3 open slots27Three best other approved qualifications, potentially including second English

Example: English Language 7, English Literature 8, Maths 8, Biology 9, Chemistry 8, Physics 8, History 7, French 6, Religious Studies 9 and Music 8. English = 16; Maths = 16; EBacc subjects = 25 (Biology 9, Chemistry 8, Physics 8); open = 24 (RE 9, Music 8, English Language 7). Attainment 8 = 81.

Religious Education does not qualify for an EBacc subject slot but can contribute a full nine points in an Attainment 8 open slot. This is how a strong RE result can help Michaela's Attainment 8 without affecting its EBacc APS. A language is not mandatory for Attainment 8 if the EBacc slots can be filled with other qualifying subjects.

Detailed official rules cover combined-science point averaging, discounting/overlapping subjects, early entry, technical qualifications and non-GCSE points. Our calculator models ordinary numbered GCSE grades, excluding discounting and AS-level exceptions.

03 · PROGRESS

Progress 8: achievement relative to similar starting points

DfE groups pupils by their KS2 English reading and maths starting attainment, finds the national Attainment 8 average for comparable pupils, and compares each pupil's Attainment 8 result with that benchmark. The difference is divided by 10 (the ten weighted grade positions). A school's score is the average of individual pupil scores, with official technical adjustments.

(Pupil Attainment 8 − national expected Attainment 8 for comparable KS2 attainment) ÷ 10
Illustrative Progress 81.2

This is a demonstration, not an official Progress 8 prediction: actual national benchmarks depend on a child's KS2 results, examination cohort and official adjustments. Progress 8 was not calculated for 2024/25 or 2025/26 because the relevant KS2 tests were cancelled during the pandemic. An unavailable value is not zero progress.

How to read it: 0 = in line with similarly prepared pupils nationally; +0.5 = roughly half a GCSE grade above expectation per weighted grade position; −0.5 = below. Comparisons should also consider official confidence intervals and the composition of the cohort.

04 · SUBJECT COMBINATIONS

EBacc: five subject areas, six grade positions

The English Baccalaureate is not a separate examination or certificate. Historically the DfE reported the share of pupils taking its prescribed combination, the share meeting achievement thresholds and an Average Point Score (APS).

AreaEntry patternAPS positions
EnglishBoth English Language and LiteratureBetter grade of the two, only when both entered
MathsApproved Mathematics GCSEOne Maths grade
ScienceCombined Science double award or eligible set of three separate sciences (Biology, Chemistry, Physics, Computer Science)Two Combined Science grade positions or best two separate-science grades, subject to the three-science entry rule
HumanitiesHistory or GeographyBetter eligible grade
LanguageFrench, Spanish, German, Latin or another approved languageBest eligible language grade

That can mean six GCSE qualifications with Combined Science (English Language, English Literature, Maths, the Combined Science double award, humanities and language), producing seven awarded GCSE grade positions but only six EBacc APS positions. A three-separate-science route involves even more GCSE entries. The EBacc APS divides the six component points by six. English and Maths are not double weighted here.

(English + Maths + Science grade 1 + Science grade 2 + Humanities + Language) ÷ 6

Example: English best grade 8, Maths 8, Biology 9, Chemistry 8, History 7 and French 6: (8 + 8 + 9 + 8 + 7 + 6) ÷ 6 = 7.67 EBacc APS (with a valid separate-science entry set). Missing a language contributes zero to that pillar, even if RE is grade 9.

The DfE removed EBacc headline and additional measures from the 2025/26 accountability release. Historical results for 2022/23–2024/25 remain relevant for comparing past cohorts; they must be labelled by year. Published subject entries and individual subject grades may still be available, but a new official school EBacc APS is not produced in that same way.

05 · THE OTHER APS

APS is not one universally defined score

APS simply means Average Point Score. Always check which grades are included and how they are weighted. An EBacc APS is a DfE historical accountability measure; a sixth-form GCSE APS is an admissions calculation; A-level APS is a different system using post-16 qualifications.

For example, St Dominic's Sixth Form College's admissions approach averages the numeric grades of all GCSEs/IGCSEs counted under its policy, without double weighting English or Maths. Ten grades adding to 78 yield a GCSE APS of 7.8. English, Maths and the number of grade 5+ subjects still matter independently, as do subject-specific course requirements and oversubscription arrangements. It is not Attainment 8 ÷ 10.

St Dominic's-style GCSE APS = sum of eligible GCSE/IGCSE numeric grades ÷ number of grades

      07 · WHAT TO WATCH

      Comparing schools fairly

      Missing ≠ zero

      Progress 8 may be unavailable because KS2 baseline tests were cancelled. A blank score should not be treated as weak performance.

      IGCSE recognition

      Independent schools may use IGCSE courses that are not recognised in DfE school accountability calculations. Their published grade 9–7 results and DfE Attainment 8 are not always directly comparable.

      RE counts differently

      RE and Music can score in the Attainment 8 open bucket, but neither counts towards historical EBacc APS or EBacc entry.

      Three different denominators

      Attainment 8 averages weighted points across school cohorts; EBacc APS uses six subject positions; sixth-form GCSE APS uses the applicant's counted GCSE grades.
