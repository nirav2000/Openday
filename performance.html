What the columns mean

Grade 9 / Grades 9–7 show the number and percentage of GCSE grade awards in those bands, derived from the latest published DfE subject-grade counts. Combined Science counts as two GCSE awards. Where DfE suppresses small grade cells, the derived percentage can be slightly understated.

Columns lets you choose which metrics appear and move them into your preferred order. That layout is remembered on this device; Reset default restores Grade 9 and Grades 9–7 near the front.

Attainment 8 is the average points score across eight qualifying subjects.

English & maths 5+ is the percentage achieving grade 5 or above in both.

Progress 8 compares progress with pupils nationally who had similar KS2 starting points. It is unavailable for 2024/25 and 2025/26 because those cohorts lack KS2 baselines after COVID disruption.

Whole-school FSM / EAL come from the January 2026 school census. EAL means first language known or believed to be other than English.

SEN support / EHCP are whole-school January 2026 DfE SEN census percentages.

Absence is the full-year percentage of possible sessions missed; PA is the percentage persistently absent (10% or more sessions missed). Latest full-year data is 2024/25.

A-level APS is the average point score per A-level entry; the DfE also supplies the corresponding average grade.

Subject details are published grade counts, ordered highest grade first. Each grade is boxed for quick scanning. Combined Science pairs such as 9–8 are double-award outcomes rather than a different grading scale. Small counts may be suppressed by DfE.
