How to read the results: A school percentage is the proportion of Year 6 pupils meeting that standard—not a test mark. In 2025, England averaged 62% for combined expected and 8% for combined higher. Higher percentage is generally better, but compare the same measure and same year. The highest possible proportion is 100%; the lowest is 0%.
